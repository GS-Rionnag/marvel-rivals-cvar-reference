# General `r.*` CVar batch E: depth of field and renderer diagnostics

This guide documents the next 25 currently uncovered registered names with exact first dotted component `r.`, after batches A–D and other local/public explanatory tables. The entries cover depth-of-field blur/recombine/scatter setup, D3D11/D3D12 diagnostics, force-LOD warning metadata, and safe-zone visualization.

The `var`/`ref` kind and type come from the supplied community `console_vars.tsv`. Values from `CVarDump.txt` are a captured ShakeKill snapshot with unknown capture procedure and build; they are not treated as defaults. Epic UE 5.9 default values are separate upstream references and do not establish the game's effective defaults or implementation. Help text is attributed to its source and is not independently tested.

The static address scan is for Marvel Rivals Shipping build `1.1.4042190`. `direct-memory-candidate` means an instruction targets the registered reference address; it does not identify reads versus writes or prove execution. `address-taken` matches are counted separately. These hits are static leads, not runtime behavior evidence. Startup override acceptance and visual effects remain unverified.

## Source differences and limits

- Snapshot values differ from Epic UE 5.9 defaults for `r.DOF.Kernel.MaxBackgroundRadius`, `r.DOF.Kernel.MaxForegroundRadius`, `r.DOF.Recombine.Quality`, `r.DOF.Scatter.BackgroundCompositing`, `r.DOF.Scatter.ForegroundCompositing`, and `r.DOF.TemporalAAQuality`. These comparisons do not show which value the game uses at runtime.
- Help for both `r.DOF.Recombine.EnableBokehSettings` and `r.DOF.Recombine.Quality` labels a value as default that differs from the Epic 5.9 reference: 1 versus 0 for the bokeh toggle, and 2 versus 1 for quality. The supplied capture has `r.DOF.Recombine.EnableBokehSettings=0` and `r.DOF.Recombine.Quality=0`; none of these sources establishes the game's effective defaults.
- `r.DOF.Recombine.SkipEmptyClamp`, the two scatter-isolation controls, and `r.DOF.Setup.DownsampleCocMethod` have no snapshot or Epic default in the registry catalog. `r.DOF.Scatter.IsolationCocRadiusRatio` and `r.DOF.Scatter.IsolationMinNeighborCount` have help-specified defaults, which are not proven game defaults.
- The DX11 and DX12 NVIDIA Aftermath wait-time help does not state units. Their names and defaults do not establish NVIDIA hardware requirements, feature initialization, or crash-dump behavior for this game.
- The build scan lists `ref_rva` matches for the six `ref` entries: 19 `direct-memory-candidate` instruction hits and 6 `address-taken` hits total. The 19 `var` entries have no listed `ref_rva` matches; their `obj_rva` has 57 `direct-memory-candidate` and 19 `address-taken` hits. Across all 25 entries, `obj_rva` totals 71 `direct-memory-candidate` and 25 `address-taken` matches. These are not directional or runtime conclusions.
- No entry here is confirmed as startup-overridable or runtime-editable in Marvel Rivals. No game launch or visual test was performed.
