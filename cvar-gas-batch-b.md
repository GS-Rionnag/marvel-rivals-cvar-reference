## Evidence limits and coverage audit

- The 16 selected names have 16 source rows with no conflicting kind, type, or help metadata. Fifteen have captured snapshot values; `GAS.Rogue106521.UseRootCapsuleTarget` has no captured value. No exact-name Epic UE 5.9 default is recorded for these names.
- The build scan produced 107 hits: 78 `direct-memory-candidate` and 29 `address-taken`. Counts do not establish runtime reads/writes, control-flow execution, accepted startup overrides, or gameplay effects. A `var` row with no `ref_rva` match still has `obj_rva` evidence; that does not establish runtime value consumption.
- These 16 entries do not overlap batch A or earlier explanatory tables. The three sources of coverage—40 names in earlier guides, 25 in batch A, and 16 here—cover all 81 unique exact-case `GAS.*` registered `var`/`ref` names. The current uncovered count is zero.
- `GAS.SetGERepMode`’s supplied help enumerates modes 1–6 while the recorded snapshot is 0; the available sources do not explain this difference.
