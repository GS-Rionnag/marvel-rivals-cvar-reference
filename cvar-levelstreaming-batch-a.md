## Evidence limits

- The source registry classifies all eight as `ref`, not `var`. Treat these as CVar references/candidates; registration and external editability are not established.
- Snapshot entries are captured values, not recommended or guaranteed defaults. `LevelStreaming.Profiling.Enabled.Shipping` and `LevelStreaming.Profiling.StartAutomatically` are false in the snapshot; this does not establish that profiling is unavailable.
- Epic UE 5.9 exact-name reference matches exist for some rows only. Matching defaults are a comparison source, not proof of the game’s engine version or game defaults.
- Where snapshot and Epic UE 5.9 values differ, retain both as distinct evidence; the game-build inventory has no runtime value measurement.
- Printable strings and static address-reference scan counts do not establish registration, reads/writes, or execution.
- No controlled test established whether the mod loader accepts these in `[Overrides]` or their gameplay effects.
