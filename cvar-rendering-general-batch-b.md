# Rendering general CVar batch B: occlusion, platform, and async controls

This batch documents 25 `r.*` names found in the preserved community `var`/`ref` registry. Its captured values are snapshots, not verified Marvel Rivals defaults. The registry's Epic 5.9 exact-name comparison is upstream context; it does not establish the game's fork behavior. The local static scan targets Marvel Rivals Shipping build `1.1.4042190`.

## Interpretation

All 25 names have `var` or `ref` rows in the community registry and exact name strings in the scanned Shipping executable. Seven have direct `ref_rva` read candidates (`r.AllowHighQualityCharacterShadow`, `r.AllowOcclusionQueries`, `r.AllowPointLightCubemapShadows`, `r.AllowSimpleLights`, `r.AllowSubPrimitiveQueries`, `r.AlwaysPrepareGlobalDistaneField`, and `r.AsyncCacheMeshDrawCommands`). `r.AllowPrecomputedVisibility` has an address-taking match only. The other 17 have no `ref_rva` value-read address in this registry scan; object-address setup matches do not prove use of their values. This is static evidence, not a runtime acceptance or behavior test.

The seven `r.Android.*`/Android rendering controls are platform-specific by their descriptions. Their strings appearing in the Windows executable does not establish that they affect Windows gameplay. The example loader syntax can express all 25 as `name=value` lines, but no controlled startup-override test established acceptance, priority, or visible/performance effect. Do not treat snapshot values or upstream defaults as recommendations.
