## Evidence and override limits

- The 23 names above each have exactly one `var` or `ref` entry in [`console_vars.tsv`](https://discord.com/channels/1512820805615091712/1512825591529869383/1570970983353950322). The recorded snapshot values come from [`CVarDump.txt`](https://discord.com/channels/1512820805615091712/1512825591529869383/1562934699502866452); three names have no snapshot line. The dump's build and collection method are unknown.
- The build-specific scan is [`build-4042190-cvar-address-hits.tsv`](EVIDENCE.md#evidence-artifacts-not-included), for Marvel Rivals Shipping build `1.1.4042190`. Direct reference-value candidates were found for 9 of the 23 entries: `ScopeApplyInterval`, `ScopeApplyInterval.UseTraceTime`, `ScopedTable.UseCharacterResolver`, `Select.ArbiterAngleThreshold`, `Select.EnableArbiter`, `ServerRPCBatching.Log`, `SyncLoadTableIfNotExist`, `TimeBudgetForMarvelAbilityAgentRecycle`, and `UseReplicationConditionForActiveGameplayEffects`. The remaining fourteen had no direct reference-value candidate in this saved scan. This scan limitation does not prove that no indirect use exists.
- Setup/address-taking hits, strings near a CVar name, and static reference reads do not by themselves demonstrate runtime behavior, user-visible effects, or server/client authority. Translations of Chinese help for `Select.ArbiterAngleThreshold` and both `TimeBudgetForMarvelAbilityAgentRecycle` entries are assistant translations marked unreviewed in [`cvar-chinese-help-translations.md`](cvar-chinese-help-translations.md).
- A registry row, captured value, help string, or static read candidate does not prove that the supplied loader accepts a particular override, that it survives game initialization, or that changing it produces a safe or intended in-game result. No overrides or runtime behavior were tested for this batch.
