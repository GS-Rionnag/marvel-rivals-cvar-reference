# Networked physics predictive-interpolation CVars

This batch covers the first 25 uncovered registered `np2.PredictiveInterpolation.*` CVars. They control correction/extrapolation and diagnostics for network-replicated physics. The descriptions reflect the community registry or exact-name Epic UE 5.9 reference; neither confirms the game’s engine revision, live values, or actual network behavior.

Build evidence comes from static address-reference scanning of Marvel Rivals Shipping build `1.1.4042190`. Every row is a `ref` candidate in the supplied registry. A direct-memory candidate may be a read, comparison, or write; the scan does not identify the branch or call path. No live override or controlled network/physics test was performed.

## Interpretation notes

- Predictive interpolation trades correction smoothness and prediction against snap artifacts and accumulated error. Individual controls describe how those mechanisms are parameterized, but their combined effect depends on runtime physics/network state.
- `np2.PredictiveInterpolation.ErrorAccAngVelMaxLimit` has a source wording difference: community help describes its angular-velocity threshold in radians, while the Epic 5.9 text says degrees. This guide preserves both and does not resolve the unit mismatch.
- Snapshots are captured values, not recommended values. Epic 5.9 defaults are upstream references only.
- These controls affect networked simulation and may change gameplay behavior. No claim is made that the loader accepts them or that a setting is safe to change.
