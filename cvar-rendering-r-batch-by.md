# GPU rendering CVars: volumetric fog, render targets, and voxels (batch BY)

This guide documents the next 25 currently uncovered unique `r.*` registry names after batch BX: 12 volumetric-fog controls, two volumetric-lightmap visualization controls, seven volumetric render-target controls, and three voxel controls. Duplicate source rows are counted once by exact CVar name. Supplied community help is summarized below; blank and vague descriptions remain unresolved.

Community help and captured values do not prove live-game implementation or `.ini` acceptance. Snapshot values are observations, not defaults or recommendations. Epic UE 5.9 reference values were not supplied for this batch. Static address-reference hits in Shipping build 1.1.4042190 are candidates only, not proof of runtime reads, writes, execution, or visible effects.

## Interpretation and limits

- The descriptions are community-supplied help. Several lines are vague or contain apparent typos; they are not corrected into stronger claims.
- Captured values are observations rather than recommendations or verified defaults for the current game build.
- `var`/`ref` classifications and address-reference candidates do not establish that a startup `[Overrides]` line is accepted by Marvel Rivals.
- Address scans can reflect initialization, writes, or address passing. They do not prove runtime execution or visible effects.
