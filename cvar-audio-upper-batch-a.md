# Audio CVar batch A

This guide covers the first 25 alphabetically sorted uncovered registered names whose exact, case-sensitive first dotted component is `Audio`. This prefix is distinct from lowercase `au`: the supplied registry contains 30 `Audio.*` names and 139 `au.*` names, and no `au.*` entry is included here. No earlier local or public explanatory table covered an exact `Audio.*` name. Five `Audio.*` names remain after this batch.

“Snapshot” is the value captured in the supplied registry catalog; it is not assumed to be a default or recommendation. The Epic UE 5.9 column shows exact-name upstream reference defaults; `—` means no matching default was recorded. English renderings of Chinese help are labeled as unreviewed assistant translations. Help describes supplied intent, not verified live behavior.

## Evidence limits and coverage audit

- The 25 entries have one consistent registry row each: 9 are bool `ref`, 4 are float `ref`, 4 are int32 `ref`, 3 are bool `var`, 4 are int32 `var`, and 1 is FString `ref`. Snapshots are present for 23; `Audio.DisabledBanks` and `Audio.EnableHeroVoiceConfigGenerationCache` have no captured value. No exact-name Epic UE 5.9 default is recorded for these 25.
- Static scan totals are 113 `direct-memory-candidate` hits and 45 `address-taken` hits. A candidate may be a read, write, comparison, or initialization; address-taking does not show a value access. These matches do not prove execution, startup-override acceptance, `.ini` editability, or a live audio effect.
- No exact `Audio.*` names appeared in earlier local or public explanatory tables. This batch covers the first 25 of 30; five remain. Lowercase `au.*` is a separate component and is not counted toward this family.
- The `EnableAkAuxBusLoad` and `EnableAkEventLoad` names look boolean-like, but their supplied types are `float` and their help is empty; the guide therefore leaves their meaning unresolved.
