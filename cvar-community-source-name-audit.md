The 2025 Steam thread also contains a Jan 6 comment claiming that its `.ini` tweaks no longer worked. That comment does not identify which file or settings were tested, so it is a conflicting community report rather than a result for any individual key. The older Steam optimization guide cited above is currently marked removed/incompatible on Steam. The discussion posts are mutable and have no revision identifiers; their post dates and specific rendered lines are recorded above. Natram's file is pinned to commit [`b243da3`](https://github.com/Natram1zh/Marvel-Rivals-Config/commit/b243da3aee605f80ca0e6abfa383793c069a46b9). Noverse's files are pinned to commit [`cc18694`](https://github.com/nohuto/game-tools/commit/cc18694f68c7eab3b15ab7f2bc63de42b0da211e); the project is archived, and its description disclaims testing. These source conditions and the lack of runtime tests limit the findings to name-presence evidence.

## Scope and counting

The distinct unverified outside-registry candidate count is now 135: the corrected prior total of 121 plus 14 names found in the Jan 5, 2025 Steam discussion reply. The two source spellings that case-fold to already registered canonical names are recorded for auditability but do not add distinct candidates. Raw case-sensitive community strings are separate from the candidate count.
