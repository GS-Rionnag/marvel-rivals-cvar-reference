# Physics general and movement CVar batch A

This guide covers the first 25 alphabetically sorted currently undocumented registered `p.*` variables. The group crosses actor movement, physics collision/overlap, animation dynamics, and projectile-related controls. Supplied help is the author’s stated purpose, not confirmation of a live match effect.

Snapshot values are observations; Epic UE 5.9 defaults are upstream exact-name references. Static address candidates are from Marvel Rivals Shipping build `1.1.4042190`; they are not complete control-flow traces. No config override or gameplay test was performed.

## Interpretation limits

- Entries with empty help are not defined from their names alone.
- A UE 5.9 default does not establish game-build behavior or version alignment.
- A `var`/`ref` row and binary address match do not prove startup `.ini` acceptance or player-facing editability.
