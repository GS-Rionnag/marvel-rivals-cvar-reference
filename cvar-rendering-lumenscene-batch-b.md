## Interpretation limits

- `r.LumenScene.FarField.ReferencePos.Z` names an axis-specific far-field reference position, but the supplied help and static scan do not define its coordinate space or downstream behavior.
- Global SDF descriptions explain threshold and scaling concepts at the UE reference level. They do not validate a quality, performance, or geometry tradeoff in Marvel Rivals.
- Heightfield rows marked `var` have object-address scan hits but no `ref_rva` value-read candidate in this scan. The registry and UE help describe intended controls; they do not show that the game reads changed values.
- `var`/`ref` registry kind, a captured snapshot, and a static address hit do not prove that an `.ini` startup override is accepted or has an in-game effect. Snapshot values are not recommendations.
