## Evidence limits

- Snapshot values are observations from the supplied `CVarDump.txt`, not verified game defaults or recommendations.
- UE 5.9 values are exact-name upstream reference data and do not establish the game’s engine revision. The community CVar dump’s game-build alignment is also unconfirmed.
- Build scan rows are instructions referencing the community registry’s `ref_rva` and `obj_rva` fields in Shipping build 1.1.4042190. Direct-memory and address-taking labels are candidate classifications; they do not prove a read, write, execution path, registration, or visible effect.
- `s.Summoned.LifeTime` has a source-described format but no captured snapshot; its parsing and semantics remain unclear.
- No controlled runtime test establishes whether any entry is accepted through the loader’s `[Overrides]` section or changes behavior.

Sources: [community CVar registry](cvar-community-registry.md), [Epic UE 5.9 defaults and captured values](cvar-community-registry.md), [preserved community attachments](EVIDENCE.md#community-sources), and [build-specific address-reference scan](EVIDENCE.md#build-specific-static-analysis).
