Community `var`/`ref` help describes the source author’s stated purpose; it does not confirm behavior in the current game. Snapshot values come from the supplied `CVarDump.txt`; Epic UE 5.9 defaults and descriptions are exact-name upstream references, not established Marvel Rivals defaults. Static address matches below are from Shipping build `1.1.4042190`. Direct-memory candidates may represent reads, writes, comparisons, or initialization; address-taking matches alone do not establish a value read. No runtime or startup override test was performed.

## Interpretation limits

- Community help is reported as supplied; any Chinese translation is labeled unreviewed. Epic descriptions/defaults are a separate upstream reference.
- Registration and a dump value do not establish startup `.ini` acceptance, player editability, or a live gameplay effect.
- Static address matches are leads, not complete value-use traces. No saved hit does not establish that a CVar is unused.
