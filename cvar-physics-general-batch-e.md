## Interpretation limits

- The registered `var`/`ref` kind and its presence in a supplied dump do not establish that a player can change it through an `.ini` override or that the current game honors it.
- Community help is reported as source help. Chinese-source renderings are explicitly marked unreviewed. Exact-name Epic help is labeled separately.
- A static scanner hit is an address match, not a complete trace of when or how the value is consumed. Absence of a hit also does not prove the CVar is unused.
