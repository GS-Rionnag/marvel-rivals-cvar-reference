Snapshot values and exact-name Epic UE 5.9 reference defaults are separate. Build address evidence comes from static scanning of Marvel Rivals Shipping build `1.1.4042190`; direct-memory candidates are not full data-flow traces. No controlled config or ability test was performed.

## Interpretation limits

- Chinese help translations are marked as unreviewed; the original registry text is the source.
- Missing help and missing Epic defaults remain unknown rather than inferred from names.
- Static references do not establish runtime execution, editability, or an in-match effect.
