## Evidence limits

- Snapshot values are observations from the supplied `CVarDump.txt`, not verified defaults or recommendations.
- Epic defaults are exact-name UE 5.9 reference matches only; they do not prove that the game uses UE 5.9. A dash means the catalog records no exact default.
- Help comes from the community registry attachment. The meanings above condense that text; the one help-free row is marked as name-based uncertainty.
- Build scan counts are references to the registry `ref_rva` and `obj_rva` fields in the installed 1.1.4042190 executable. Direct-memory candidates and address-taking instructions were not control-flow validated here; neither class proves runtime reads, writes, activation, or player-visible effects.
- No controlled runtime test established whether the entries are accepted through the loader's `[Overrides]` section or what happens if changed.

Sources: [community CVar registry](cvar-community-registry.md), [Epic UE 5.9 comparison and snapshots](cvar-community-registry.md), [preserved community attachments](EVIDENCE.md#community-sources), and [build-specific address-reference scan](EVIDENCE.md#build-specific-static-analysis).
