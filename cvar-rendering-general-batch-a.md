# Rendering CVar batch A: AMD, ACEScg HDR, occlusion, and misc controls

This batch covers 25 rendering variables from the community `var`/`ref` registry. The registry attachment does not identify its game build. Values below come from its captured `CVarDump.txt` snapshot and are not assumed to be defaults or recommendations. Build-specific evidence refers to Marvel Rivals Shipping `1.1.4042190` and the saved x64 address-reference scan.

## What the static scan establishes

All 25 names occur in the selected Shipping-build CVar string inventory. That establishes literal presence only. The stronger address-reference evidence is concentrated in the six float parameters under `r.AcescgHdr`, five `r.ActorOcclusion.*` references, `r.AllowCachedUniformExpressions`, and `r.AllowDepthBoundsTest`:

- The six `r.AcescgHdr` floats are loaded from their `ref_rva` addresses in a short code region (`0x141F84CD5` through `0x141F84D38`). The group relationship is supported by the neighboring instructions; the transfer function, units, and gate are not.
- The five `r.ActorOcclusion.*` settings have value-read candidates for their bounds, enable, invalid-result, or visualization behavior. The community help aligns with those reads, but no complete caller/control-flow trace or in-game observation was made.
- `r.AllowCachedUniformExpressions` and `r.AllowDepthBoundsTest` have direct comparison candidates. The latter's two comparisons and the former's one comparison show the values are checked in compiled code, but not their full effects.
- For the other 12 entries, this scan found object-address setup candidates rather than direct reads of the value. That is inconclusive: no direct read was observed by this scan, not proof the setting is unused.

These are build-specific static findings, not proof that startup overrides are accepted, that the referenced code executes in a normal match, or that changing a setting produces a visible or beneficial result. Some entries explicitly describe startup-only device initialization; most have no controlled override test.
