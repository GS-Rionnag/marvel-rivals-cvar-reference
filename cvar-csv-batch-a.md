# CSV profiling CVar batch A

This guide documents all 11 currently uncovered exact-case lower-case `csv.*` variable/reference names. The descriptions concern CSV capture, output/compression, frame-rate reporting, or wait/stat collection. The game’s Shipping executable string inventory includes all eleven names, but name presence alone does not prove that these settings are registered or used at runtime.

## Evidence limits

- Snapshot values are observations from `CVarDump.txt`, not verified defaults or recommendations.
- Epic defaults are exact-name UE 5.9 reference matches. They do not establish the game’s engine version; the Shipping-only variable has no recorded UE 5.9 match.
- The game-build inventory proves only printable-name presence. Static address counts are references to registry fields, not confirmed reads, writes, registration, or execution.
- `csv.Shipping.ContinuousWrites` contains a spelling discrepancy in its help target; do not assume the misspelled alias is real without runtime or source confirmation.
- The tracking CVars explicitly warn of overhead. This guide does not establish loader acceptance, safe values, or runtime effects.
- No controlled runtime test established whether any entry is accepted through the loader’s `[Overrides]` section.
