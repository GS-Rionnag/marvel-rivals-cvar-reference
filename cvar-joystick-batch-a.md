## Evidence limits and coverage audit

- All 25 names have one consistent registry row and kind `var`: 23 are float and 2 are bool. Every entry has a captured snapshot, and none has an exact-name Epic UE 5.9 default in the catalog.
- The static scan reports 75 `direct-memory-candidate` hits and 25 `address-taken` hits across the 25 `obj_rva` values; the entries have no `ref_rva` matches. Candidates do not establish read/write direction or execution. These matches do not prove the CVar is editable in a startup config or that changing it affects current gameplay.
- No name overlaps a prior local or public explanatory table. The batch contains the first 25 of 28 unique `Joystick.*` registered names, leaving three for a later batch.
- `Joystick.PushRangeThreshold` is the one clear help/snapshot mismatch in this slice: help says a default of `3`, while the catalog records snapshot `0.2`. The sources do not establish whether the snapshot is overridden, from a different build, or otherwise explained.
