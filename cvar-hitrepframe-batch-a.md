## Evidence limits and coverage audit

- The 25 names each have one consistent registry row: 20 are int32, 3 are FString, and 2 are float; all have kind `var`. Snapshots are present for 22; the three FString entries have no captured value. No exact-name Epic UE 5.9 default is recorded for any of the 25.
- The build scan has 52 `direct-memory-candidate` hits and 25 `address-taken` hits across these names. Since each is a `var` with `ref_rva=0`, the scan reports object-address (`obj_rva`) hits only. Scanner candidates do not establish reads versus writes, executed code, runtime effects, startup override acceptance, or `.ini` editability.
- The entries do not overlap earlier explanatory tables. There are 10 additional uncovered registered `HitRepFrame.*` names after this first batch of 25.
