# General `r.*` CVar batch H: diffuse indirect and distance fields

This guide documents the first 25 currently uncovered registered names with exact first dotted component `r.`, using the focused local/public explanatory-guide tables as coverage and excluding inventory-only catalogs (including `cvar-third-party-config-crosscheck.md`). It covers diffuse-indirect controls, render-feature switches, fade behavior, and distance-field ambient occlusion/shadowing/atlas settings.

The `var`/`ref` kinds and types come from the supplied community `console_vars.tsv`. The `CVarDump.txt` values are a captured ShakeKill snapshot with unknown capture method and game build; they are observations, not documented defaults. Epic UE 5.9 values are upstream reference data and do not prove what Marvel Rivals applies. Community help is presented as supplied source text, not independently tested.

Static address evidence comes from the Marvel Rivals Shipping build `1.1.4042190`. `direct-memory-candidate` hits are instruction-address matches, not confirmed reads/writes or proof of runtime execution. `address-taken` hits are counted separately. No startup override acceptance or in-game effect was tested.

## Source differences and limits

- Snapshot values differ from Epic 5.9 defaults for `r.DiffuseIndirectOffUseDepthBoundsAO`, `r.DistanceFieldAO`, `r.DistanceFields.BlockAllocatorSizeInBricks`, `r.DistanceFields.BrickAtlasMaxSizeZ`, and `r.DistanceFields.BrickAtlasSizeXYInBricks`. These are captured-value comparisons, not proof of the game's effective defaults.
- `r.DiffuseIndirect.HalfRes` and `r.DiffuseIndirect.RayPerPixel` have `TODO(Guillaume)` source help, and `r.DistanceFields.BoundCullingSize` has no help. The value/type and name alone do not define their effect.
- `r.DisableMaterialShaderCompile` is a `ref` row with one address-taking scan hit but no `ref_rva` direct-memory candidate. The static match does not support a value-use claim for this entry.
- The scan reports ten `ref` rows with `ref_rva` address hits: 29 `direct-memory-candidate` instructions and 10 `address-taken` hits in aggregate. Across all 25 names, `obj_rva` totals are 74 `direct-memory-candidate` and 25 `address-taken` hits. These are static leads only; they do not confirm registration or execution in the scanned build.
- No startup override acceptance or live rendering effect is established. No in-game test was performed.
