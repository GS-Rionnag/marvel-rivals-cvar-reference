# Niagara and particle-system CVars: FX batch B

This batch covers the next 25 uncovered registered `fx.*` variables after the FX budget and Cascade entries in [FX budget and Cascade CVars](cvar-fx-budget-batch-a.md). The community registry supplies help and a captured-value snapshot; the snapshot's game build and collection method are unknown, so values here are observations rather than verified game defaults or recommendations.

## What the scan establishes

All 25 names have `ref` rows in the supplied registry and appear in the saved build-specific address scan. Fourteen entries have at least one direct-memory candidate that targets their `ref_rva`: `fx.DeferrPSCDeactivation`, `fx.DumpParticleData`, `fx.DumpSystemData`, `fx.EnableNiagaraCRHandler`, `fx.EnableNiagaraRibbonRendering`, `fx.EnableNiagaraSpriteRendering`, `fx.ExecVMScripts`, `fx.FXAllowParticleMeshLODs`, `fx.ForceExecVMPath`, `fx.GPUSimulationTextureSizeX`, `fx.GPUSimulationTextureSizeY`, and the three `fx.GPUSort.*` controls. These are static references in build 1.1.4042190; they do not prove runtime registration, startup-config acceptance, or an in-game effect. The remaining entries have registry/help and object/setup evidence, without a direct value access found by this scan.

The `ref` label is not by itself proof that changing a value in a startup `.ini` reaches the relevant code. Niagara renderer toggles can remove whole renderer types, and the compilation/VM controls are diagnostic or experimental. The texture dimensions and sort-buffer controls may also be affected by project settings or device profiles. No controlled override or in-game comparison was performed for this batch.
