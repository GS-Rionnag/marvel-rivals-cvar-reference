## Interpretation limits

- Registration and a saved snapshot do not show that the game accepts a startup `.ini` override or that a player can edit the setting.
- Community help is reported as supplied; Chinese renderings are marked unreviewed. Epic descriptions/defaults are kept as separate upstream evidence.
- Static address matches are not full runtime traces. No match in this scan does not prove a CVar is unused.
