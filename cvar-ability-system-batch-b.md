# AbilitySystem CVar batch B

This page covers 25 AbilitySystem entries from the preserved community registry. Registry `ref` and `var` labels, help strings, and dump values are static attachment evidence; the attachment does not identify its capture build or method. Snapshot values are observations, not defaults or recommendations. The local code-reference evidence is from Marvel Rivals Shipping build `1.1.4042190` and its saved x64 address scan.

## Evidence and editability limits

- All 25 exact spellings have `var` or `ref` rows in [`console_vars.tsv`](https://discord.com/channels/1512820805615091712/1512825591529869383/1570970983353950322). All are absent from the exact-name Epic 5.9 CVar reference; the community registry's Epic comparison column is blank for these entries. Their help is therefore game/fork/community metadata, not upstream Unreal documentation.
- The captured values above come from [`CVarDump.txt`](https://discord.com/channels/1512820805615091712/1512825591529869383/1562934699502866452). `MaxDelayForMarvelAbilityAgentRecycle` has no snapshot line. None of these values is established as a Marvel Rivals default.
- The local address scan is preserved in [`build-4042190-cvar-address-hits.tsv`](EVIDENCE.md#evidence-artifacts-not-included). For 21 entries, it contains at least one direct-memory candidate to the registered `ref_rva`; instruction samples above are loads or comparisons. Four entries have no direct value-read candidate in this scan: `SharedInLevels`, `PreAllocatedCueActorLifeSpanDebug`, `Prediction.ActivationBuffer`, and `RPCQueue.ServerLoopTryExecuteLogicTime`. Indirect access or scan limitations remain possible. Object-address setup writes are not CVar reads.
- Four Chinese help strings have unreviewed translations in [`cvar-chinese-help-translations.md`](cvar-chinese-help-translations.md): the two GameplayCue budgets, the agent-recycle deadline, and the penetrable-object collision option. The original Chinese text and translation should be checked by a fluent reader before relying on fine distinctions.
- A CVar row, snapshot value, or static read candidate does not prove a startup loader accepts an override or that a gameplay outcome changes. No override was tested. In particular, `IgnoreCooldowns`'s help does not prove that a client can alter server-authoritative cooldown behavior.
