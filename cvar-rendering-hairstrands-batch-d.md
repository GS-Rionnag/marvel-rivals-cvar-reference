# HairStrands CVar batch D: mesh projection, ray tracing, and selection

This batch covers the next 25 alphabetically sorted registered `r.HairStrands.*` variables after batches A–C and all other focused explanatory tables. The entries span mesh-projection debug views, hair mesh/LOD settings, path-tracing invalidation, BSDF visualization, projection limits, rasterization, ray-tracing geometry, lighting, selection, and shadow casting.

The community dump labels entries `var` or `ref`; that source classification is not proof that a game override is editable or effective. Snapshot values are observations from the supplied CVar dump. Exact-name Epic UE 5.9 defaults are upstream references, not verified Marvel Rivals defaults. The attachments do not identify their capture build or procedure.

Static build evidence is from the address-reference scan of Marvel Rivals Shipping build `1.1.4042190`. The literal scan classes `direct-memory-candidate` and `address-taken` are counted separately. A direct-memory candidate may be a read, write, comparison, or initialization; the scan is not a traced or executed path.

## Reading the evidence

- `MeshProjection.*` community descriptions say these entries render rest/deformed guide frames, samples, or triangles. The names and help establish their diagnostic intent only; output format, gating, and runtime activation are not documented here.
- `PlotBsdf.*` are described as debug visualization controls for hair BSDF, base color/absorption, exposure, and roughness. They do not imply a normal gameplay visual adjustment.
- `r.HairStrands.Meshes`, `Raytracing`, and `Shadow.CastShadowWhenNonVisible` have upstream descriptions that indicate startup or asset/visibility conditions. Their effectiveness in this game build remains unverified.
- No config override was tested. Snapshot values are not recommendations, and static addresses do not prove that an `.ini` setting is accepted.
