“Snapshot” is the captured value in the supplied registry catalog; it is not assumed to be a default or recommendation. The Epic UE 5.9 column records exact-name upstream matches, and `—` means none was recorded. Assistant-rendered English translations are identified as unreviewed. Build evidence comes from static address-reference scanning of Marvel Rivals Shipping build `1.1.4042190`; candidate counts do not establish runtime behavior.

## Evidence limits and coverage audit

- The ten selected names each have one consistent registry row. Six have captured snapshot values; four (`monster.PoolUseActorCollision`, both projectile settings, and `monster.RuleFilterBattleSideOpt`) have no captured value. No exact-name Epic UE 5.9 default is recorded for any of the ten.
- The static scan reports 37 `direct-memory-candidate` hits and 16 `address-taken` hits for this batch. A direct-memory candidate may be a read, write, comparison, or initialization instruction; an address-taking hit is not a value access. Neither class proves executed behavior, accepted config overrides, or gameplay effects.
- These ten names do not overlap batch A or any prior local/public explanatory table. The two batches cover all 35 unique exact-case `monster.*` registered `var`/`ref` names; the remaining uncovered count is zero.
