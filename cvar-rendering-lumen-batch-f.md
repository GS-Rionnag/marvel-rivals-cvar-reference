# Lumen CVar batch F: Screen Probe Gather cache and screen traces

This guide covers the next 25 uncovered registered `r.Lumen.*` variables in name order after [batch E](cvar-rendering-lumen-batch-e.md), excluding names already documented in focused Lumen tables. The community registry and `CVarDump.txt` do not identify a capture build or method. Exact-name Unreal Engine 5.9 matches provide upstream context; they do not establish Marvel Rivals' engine revision, live value, or runtime behavior.

The local evidence comes from a static address-reference scan of Marvel Rivals Shipping build `1.1.4042190`. A direct-memory candidate means an instruction targets the registered `ref_rva`; it may be a read, comparison, or write and does not establish the surrounding control flow or an active match path. No controlled `.ini` override or in-game comparison was performed. A setting can only matter when the corresponding Lumen Screen Probe Gather path is selected and supported by the active renderer and hardware.

## Evidence and editability limits

- The 25 entries include 23 `ref` rows with at least one direct-memory candidate and two `var` rows with setup/object evidence only. Static address matches do not show that the corresponding renderer path runs during a match.
- All 25 names have exact-name UE 5.9 reference entries. Their defaults match the community snapshot in this batch, but the snapshot's build and capture method are unknown; agreement does not establish Marvel Rivals defaults.
- The settings describe Screen Probe Gather, screen-space ray tracing, radiance-cache allocation, and AO. Whether any of these paths run depends on game configuration, the selected rendering method, hardware and runtime conditions; those conditions were not checked here.
- No `.ini` override test or live console query was performed. Registry presence and static references do not prove that a loader accepts an override, that a CVar is writable, or that its changed value reaches a live match.
