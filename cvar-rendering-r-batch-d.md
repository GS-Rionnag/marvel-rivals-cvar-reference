# General `r.*` CVar batch D: distance-field shadows and depth of field

This guide documents the next 25 currently uncovered registered names whose exact first dotted component is `r.`, after batches A–C and the other local/public explanatory tables. It covers distance-field shadow sampling/culling controls and several depth-of-field (DoF) gather or game-specific depth-pass settings.

The supplied `console_vars.tsv` `var`/`ref` labels are registry metadata. `CVarDump.txt` is a captured ShakeKill snapshot with unknown capture/build provenance; its values are shown as observations, not defaults. The Epic UE 5.9 defaults are exact-name upstream reference data and do not prove that Marvel Rivals uses the same implementation. Community help is quoted or summarized as the attachment's description, not independently confirmed behavior.

The static address-reference scan is for Marvel Rivals Shipping build `1.1.4042190`. `direct-memory-candidate` counts are instruction matches to the target address, not established reads or writes; `address-taken` counts identify `lea`-style address-taking. Neither class proves runtime execution or a setting's effect. No startup-override acceptance or in-game rendering change was tested.

## Source conflicts and limits

- The captured values differ from the Epic 5.9 defaults for `r.DFShadowQuality`, `r.DOF.Gather.AccumulatorQuality`, `r.DOF.Gather.PostfilterMethod`, `r.DOF.Gather.ResolutionDivisor`, and `r.DOF.Gather.RingCount`. These are snapshot-versus-upstream comparisons, not proof of modified live settings.
- The supplied `r.DOF.Gather.EnableBokehSettings` help labels 1 as the default, while the Epic 5.9 reference lists default 0. For `r.DOF.Gather.RingCount`, community help says default 5 while the Epic reference lists 4. The sources disagree; neither is asserted as Marvel Rivals' effective default.
- `r.DFShadow.DownsampleFactor` has only a fragment of community help, while the other `r.DFShadow.*` descriptions are supplied registry help without an exact Epic 5.9 match. Those descriptions are not independently validated.
- The build scan reports `ref_rva` matches for the 18 `ref` entries: 39 `direct-memory-candidate` instruction hits and 18 `address-taken` hits in aggregate. The seven `var` entries have no listed `ref_rva` matches; their `obj_rva` has 21 `direct-memory-candidate` and 7 `address-taken` hits. Across all 25 names, the `obj_rva` totals are 70 `direct-memory-candidate` and 25 `address-taken` hits. These aggregate address matches remain static-analysis leads only.
- Nothing here establishes that a setting is writable, accepted from the supplied startup config override syntax, or honored by Marvel Rivals at startup. No visual or performance result was tested.
